Reference compositions and sources

The reference is the ground-truth composition the judge (glm-5.3-flash, thinking low) compares every model answer against. Assembled from peer-reviewed literature (Michelot & Melendez-Howell 2003, Wieland 1983/1986, Carboué & Lopez 2021) and wiki articles traced to primary sources; weakly supported items are marked disputed and penalised more softly. Groups: main — counted as omissions, trace — completeness credit, disputed — presence contested by literature.

Fly agaric (Amanita muscaria)

Muscarine as the main toxin is a myth; the actual active compounds are the isoxazoles (ibotenic acid → muscimol on drying). Amatoxins/phallotoxins only in disputed traces.

Panther cap (Amanita pantherina)

The pantherina syndrome is isoxazole-based: muscimol DOMINATES in A. pantherina (sedative phase), which is why it is considered more toxic than A. muscaria. Section Validae. Bufotenin — not confirmed.
